Support

We usually reply within two business days: vbuiltapps@gmail.com

Report someone or something unsafe

Tap the ⋯ or flag on any profile, session or message, then Report. You can also Block the member right away; they disappear from your board and chats. The owner reviews reports within 24 hours. If someone is in danger, call 911 first.

How do sessions work?

Find a session on the Board, tap Request to join, and add a note. The host approves who joins. Once you’re approved you see the exact meeting spot and the crew chat. Hosts can approve, decline or cancel at any time.

What’s free and what’s membership?

Free: browse sessions and ranges and use the free drills. Membership ($9.99/month or $99.99/year, both with a 7-day free trial) lets you post and join sessions, chat with your crew, see meeting spots and open every drill.

Restore, cancel or refund

New phone? Open Profile › Restore purchases. To cancel, go to iPhone Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Range Tribe. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Suggest a range

Open Ranges › Suggest a range. We only list established, legal ranges and check every suggestion before it appears.

Delete my account

Profile › Delete account, then type DELETE. Your profile, sessions and messages are removed right away. Or email vbuiltapps@gmail.com.

Community rules

Safety first, always. Follow range rules and RO commands. No slurs, threats or harassment. No buying or selling guns, ammo or parts. Splitting lane fees is fine; charging for instruction is not. Read the full Terms.
