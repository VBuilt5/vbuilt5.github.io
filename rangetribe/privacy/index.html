Privacy Policy

Last updated October 29, 2026

The short version

Range Tribe helps adults in Arizona meet up to shoot and train. We collect only what the app needs to do that. We don’t sell your data, we don’t show ads, and we don’t track you across other apps or websites.

What we collect

DataWhy
Email and password (passwords are stored hashed)Your account and sign-in
Call sign, optional first name, photo, bio, skill levels, interests, and a self-reported CCW badgeYour public profile, shown to other members
ZIP code, or approximate location if you tap “Use my location”Showing sessions and ranges near you. We turn your location into the nearest ZIP and city. Other members see your city, never your coordinates.
Sessions you post, join requests, crew chat messages, blocks and reportsRunning the sessions board, chat and safety tools
Your typed name and the date you accepted the waiver and TermsA record that you agreed to the safety rules
Subscription status (via Apple and RevenueCat)Unlocking membership. We never see your card details.

Who sees what

  • Other members see your profile, the sessions you host, and your messages in crews you belong to.
  • The exact meeting spot for a session is shown only to the host and members the host approved.
  • Members you block can’t see your profile, sessions or messages, and you won’t see theirs.
  • The Range Tribe owner/moderators can see reports, the reported content, and account details needed to enforce the rules.

Service providers

Accounts, sessions and chat are stored with Supabase (our database host). Subscriptions are processed by Apple and tracked by RevenueCat, which receives your Range Tribe account ID and your App Store purchase history (product, price, dates) but not your name or email. These providers process data only to run Range Tribe. If you use the app in on-device mode (the web preview), data stays in your browser on that device.

Keeping and deleting data

We keep your data while your account is open. Delete it any time in the app: Profile › Delete account. That removes your profile, sessions, join requests, messages and blocks right away. Reports you filed are kept without your name so moderators can finish reviewing them. Subscriptions are billed by Apple, so cancel in iPhone Settings › your name › Subscriptions; deleting your account doesn’t cancel a subscription. You can also email vbuiltapps@gmail.com to access, correct or delete your data.

Age

Range Tribe is for adults 18 and older. We don’t knowingly collect data from anyone under 18; if we learn we have, we delete the account.

Security

Data is sent over HTTPS. The database uses row-level security so members can only read what they’re allowed to see. No system is perfect, so please don’t put sensitive information in your bio or chat.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes in a meaningful way, we’ll tell you in the app. Questions: vbuiltapps@gmail.com.
