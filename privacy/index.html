Privacy Policy

Effective October 29, 2026

The short version

Busy Body has no accounts, no ads and no analytics. Everything you enter (your answers, meals, workouts, weigh-ins and progress photos) is stored only on your phone. We never see it, sell it or share it.

What stays on your device

  • Your plan answers: name (optional), age, sex, height, weight, goal, activity level, training preferences, joint notes, diet preferences and kids' ages if you add them.
  • Your logs: meals, water, walks, workouts, sets, weigh-ins, weekly check-ins and streaks.
  • Progress photos, if you choose to add them. Photos are resized and kept inside the app's on-device storage. They are not added to your photo library and never uploaded.

Deleting the app, or using Settings › Erase all data, permanently removes this information. Because we do not hold a copy, we cannot recover it for you. Use Settings › Export backup to keep your own copy.

Purchases

Busy Body Plus is sold through Apple's App Store. Apple processes your payment; we never see your card details. To confirm your subscription we use RevenueCat, a subscription service. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID, your device's App Store receipt and purchase history (product, price, dates). It does not receive your name, email or any health data. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Reminders

Reminders are off by default. If you turn them on, they are scheduled on your phone using local notifications. No push server is involved.

Health information

Busy Body does not connect to Apple Health and does not send health information anywhere. Calorie and macro targets are general wellness estimates, not medical advice.

Children

Busy Body is intended for adults 18 and over. Kids' ages are used only to suggest family-friendly ways to join in, and stay on your device.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes we will update the date above and describe the change in the app's release notes. Questions: vbuiltapps@gmail.com.
