Common questions

How do we pair? One of you taps “Send to your partner” to share a 6-character code. The other enters it on the pairing screen.

Why can’t I see my partner’s answer? Answers unlock once you’ve both shared. Write yours first.

Do we both need to pay? No. One subscription covers both of you.

How do I cancel? On your iPhone: Settings, your name, Subscriptions, Built Together.

How do I delete my account? In the app: Settings, Delete account. It’s immediate and permanent.

If you ever feel unsafe in your relationship, call the National Domestic Violence Hotline at 1-800-799-7233 or text START to 88788.
