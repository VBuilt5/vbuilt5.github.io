Privacy policy

Last updated November 2, 2026

Built Together is a private app for two people. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have. The short version: we keep what the app needs to work, we never sell it, and you can delete it any time from inside the app.

What we collect

  • Account details: your first name, email address, and a password (stored only as a secure hash by our database provider). If you use Sign in with Apple, we receive the name and email (or Apple’s private relay email) you choose to share.
  • What you write: your answers to daily questions and weekly prompts, your reactions, your weekly check-in scores, the date ideas you like or pass on, and the topics you turn on.
  • Pairing: which account you are paired with, invite codes, and any report or block you make.
  • Settings: your time zone, reminder time, and, if you allow notifications, a device token used to tell you when your partner has answered.
  • Subscription status: whether your couple has Premium, the plan, and when it renews or ends. Payments are handled by Apple; we never see your card details.

We do not use advertising, ad identifiers, or third-party tracking, and we do not collect your location, contacts, or photos.

Who can see your answers

Only you and the partner you pair with. Your partner sees your answer to a question only after they have shared their own. Weekly check-in scores are visible only to you. We do not read your answers except when needed to investigate a report you or your partner sends us, or when required by law.

Service providers

  • Supabase hosts our database and sign-in.
  • RevenueCat and Apple process and confirm subscriptions. RevenueCat receives an anonymous account ID and purchase details, not your answers.
  • Apple Push Notification service delivers notifications you turn on.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account exists. If you unpair, each of you keeps only your own answers. When you delete your account, your profile, answers, reactions, check-ins, and swipes are permanently deleted from our database right away; backups roll off within 30 days.

Your choices

  • Export your answers: Settings, then Export my answers.
  • Delete your account: Settings, then Delete account. This works without emailing us.
  • Turn notifications off in Settings or in iPhone Settings.

Children

Built Together is for adults 18 and older in a relationship. We do not knowingly collect data from children.
