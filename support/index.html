How is my calorie target worked out?

We use the Mifflin-St Jeor equation with your age, sex, height and weight, multiply by your activity level, then subtract a safe deficit (never more than 25% and never below a sensible floor). Every Sunday, Plus members get a check-in that nudges the target by 100 kcal based on your weight trend. Tap Settings › Edit my answers to update anything.

How do I log a meal?

On Today, tap Ate it next to the meal you ate as planned. Ate something else? Tap + Add something else and enter the calories (and protein if you know it). Tap the check again to undo.

How does the streak work?

Any day with a logged meal, a walk or a workout counts. One missed day each week (Monday to Sunday) is forgiven automatically, so a busy day doesn't wipe out your progress.

My weight went up. Is the plan broken?

Daily weight swings of 1 to 3 lb from water and salt are normal. The Progress chart shows a smoothed trend line. Watch that, not single weigh-ins. You can edit or delete any weigh-in by tapping Edit or Delete in the list.

What's free and what's in Plus?

Free: your personal plan, Today with meal logging, habits and streak, and 3 full-body workouts. Plus adds your full 2 to 5 day program, workout history and personal bests with next-weight suggestions, Sunday check-ins, progress photos, all 7 days of meals with swaps, and the grocery list.

Restore, cancel or refund

New phone? Open Settings › Restore purchases. To cancel, go to iPhone Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Busy Body. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Moving to a new phone

Your data lives on your phone. Use Settings › Export backup, save the file, then Settings › Import backup on the new phone.

Reminders aren't showing

Turn them on in Settings › Reminders and allow notifications when asked. If you said no earlier, enable them in iPhone Settings › Notifications › Busy Body.
